# Change the look of the website (fonts, colours, theme)

No coding is needed for anything on this page. Everything is done in the admin panel.

## Open the appearance studio

1. Go to `/admin/` and sign in.
2. In the left-hand menu choose **Appearance**.

The screen has two halves. On the left are the controls. On the right is your
real website, and it changes as you move each control. Nobody else sees these
changes until you publish them.

## Turn the custom design on

At the top there is one switch: **Custom design is OFF / ON**.

- **OFF** — the website keeps its original design. Nothing you choose below has
  any effect. This is the safe starting point.
- **ON** — your fonts, colours and spacing replace the original ones.

Choosing a ready-made theme turns the switch on for you.

## The six tabs

| Tab | What you change |
| --- | --- |
| **Ready themes** | Eight complete colour themes. One click changes every colour on the website. |
| **Text & fonts** | The font for normal text and a separate font for headings, plus every text size, line spacing, heading thickness and capitals. |
| **Colours** | Each colour on its own: brand colour, page background, card background, text, links, header and footer. |
| **Buttons** | Filled, outline or fully rounded; small, medium or large; corner rounding; shadow; button text colour. |
| **Spacing & shape** | Space above and below sections, how wide the content may grow, rounded corners on cards, and card shadows. |
| **Logo size** | How large the logo appears on computers and on phones. Change the logo picture itself under **Reusable sections**. |

## Check it on a phone

Above the preview there are three buttons: **Computer**, **Tablet** and
**Mobile**. They show the same page at those screen widths. Use the dropdown
beside them to preview a different page.

## Readability warnings

If a colour combination would be hard to read — for example dark grey text on a
dark background — a yellow box appears above the controls and says exactly which
colours to fix. Fix these before publishing; visitors with weaker eyesight, and
Google, both care about this.

## Save and publish

- **Save changes** keeps your work privately. You can come back to it later.
- **Publish design ↗** puts it on the live website. Only an administrator can
  publish.
- **Undo all changes** puts every setting back to the original design. You still
  need to save and publish for that to reach the website.

## Sections you can show and hide

Under **Pages**, open any page. Every section now has these buttons:

- **↑ Move up** / **↓ Move down** — change the order of the page.
- **⧉ Make a copy** — duplicate a section with all of its content.
- **◌ Hide** — keep the section and everything in it, but stop visitors seeing
  it. Choose **◉ Show again** whenever you want it back. Use this instead of
  deleting anything you might want later.
- **Delete** — remove the section for good. It asks you to confirm first.

**+ Add a section** opens a picture list of every section you can add, with a
plain explanation of what each one does.

## Pictures

Choose **Media library** in the left-hand menu.

- Drag pictures onto the upload box, or choose several at once.
- Always fill in the description. It is read aloud to visitors who cannot see
  images and helps Google understand the picture.
- **Rename** changes that description later.
- **Copy link** gives you the picture's web address.
- **Delete** removes a picture. A picture that is still used on any page or
  draft cannot be deleted — remove it from the page first.
